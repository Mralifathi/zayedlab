ZAYED ENGLISH LAB - Netlify copy (with Firebase)

1. Upload ALL files in this folder together to Netlify (index.html and the three .mp3 files).
2. In Firebase > Firestore > Rules, paste the contents of firestore.rules and press Publish.
3. Teacher: press "Teacher dashboard" and enter 221133 (then sign in with your Firebase email).
4. Students open the Netlify address. The daily panel (weather, word, proverb, nature picture)
   needs internet. Everything else works offline in the offline zip.
